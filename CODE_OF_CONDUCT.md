# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in the ARK OS project is expected to help keep its
spaces respectful, constructive, and safe for people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insults or personal
attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized attention without consent,
publishing another person's private information, and deliberately disrupting
project discussions.

Give feedback about work and ideas without attacking people. Respect differing
opinions, ask questions in good faith, and accept reasonable corrections from
maintainers. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation
when needed to protect the community. To report a concern, contact the project
maintainer through the contact method listed on the repository profile.
